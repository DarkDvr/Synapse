# Synapse v1.5.0

A small sound when the Architect has finished, and four to choose between.

## Added

- **A finished-work chime.** When the chat you are looking at goes quiet after the
  Architect worked — its last words on screen and nothing else running — one short
  sound plays. It is silent for a turn that failed and for one you stopped, because
  the sound means "done", and a stop is not a finish. A Multi run keeps it quiet
  while a worker builds; the chime comes when the Architect has read the last report
  and said its piece.
- **Four sounds, in Settings → Notices:** a two-note **Chime**, a single struck
  **Bell**, a wooden **knock**, and a **Ripple** of three light plips — plus **Off**.
  Picking one plays it, and **Play It** previews whatever the form holds.
- **The sounds are Synapse's own.** They are generated by
  `scripts\make-sounds.mjs` into `web\sounds\`, so nothing is downloaded, they
  travel with the folder, and they carry no one else's licence. Mono 16-bit WAV,
  0.3–1.2 s, peaking at −14 to −18 dBFS: meant to be noticed and talked over.

## Notes

- The setting is `notify.doneSound` in `config\synapse.config.json`; an unknown name
  falls back to the default rather than asking the server for a file that is not
  there.
- The app serves `.wav` as `audio/wav` — a wrong type is a silent failure that looks
  exactly like "the setting does nothing".
- Full suite: 308 tests.
